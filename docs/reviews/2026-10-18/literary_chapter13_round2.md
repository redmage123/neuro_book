### Copyedits (optional; do not affect the score)

- **Line 116 (recommended).** There are two issues here.
  - The reconciling sentence sits awkwardly. "it would fall short by 'at least six orders of magnitude.' On the round numbers used here, the shortfall is a factor of more than half a million" puts two figures side by side without saying why they differ, and a reader who counts will notice that half a million is under six orders. Use "Even if every nucleotide in the genome were devoted to the brain's wiring, it would fall short by a factor of more than half a million on the round numbers used here, and by 'at least six orders of magnitude' on Zador's own reckoning."
  - The paragraph is 209 words. Split before "Direct counts are consistent". In that sentence "his round number" has drifted from its referent; use "with his round number of connections".
- **Line 162 (recommended).** "It is a set of compromises struck under a few hard constraints, like the cavefish's lost eyes, a compromise struck again when the world changed." It repeats "struck", and "like" compares the brain to the eyes rather than to a compromise. Use "It is a set of compromises struck under a few hard constraints; the cavefish's lost eyes are one such compromise, struck again when the world changed."
- **Line 108.** "published in 2025, led by Eneritz Rueda-Alaña and Fernando García-Moreno among others" reads awkwardly. Use "Studies of cell types and development published in 2025, among them one led by Eneritz Rueda-Alaña and Fernando García-Moreno, suggest ..."
- **Line 12.** One "cave fish" survives ("what the cave fish had saved"). Make it "cavefish".
- **Line 96.** There is a trailing space after "[^43]".
