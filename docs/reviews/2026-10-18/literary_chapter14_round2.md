  - The social-media material runs in three movements: the surveys (line 128), the experiments (line 130), and the dispute and verdict (line 132). Wiehler opens on the experiment (line 88). Figure 14.7 opens the AI section (line 142), where it is the summary of record. The beetle sentence (line 126) makes Chapter 13's supernormal stimuli do work.
- **The expert-driven rewordings read cleanly.** Kornell, Hays, and Bjork's "guessing did as well as studying the answer for more than twice as long" (line 60) is clearer than before.

The chapter now meets the standard set by Chapters 11 to 13.

- **The spine holds throughout.** That spine is "what the brain's design implies, and how strong the evidence is". Every section has an experiment the reader can picture, and contested claims are stated as contested without hedging.
- **The handover sits at the end of the AI section.**
- **The house style holds,** with no em dashes and no British spellings.

Prose quality: 5/5. Reader interest: 5/5.

### Copyedits (optional; do not affect the score)

- **Lines 114 to 122 (recommended).** The split of the old line 100 has loosened two references.
  - Line 116, "found the opposite outcome", now sits a paragraph away from Bastani, and "opposite" could be read against the tutor that did no worse. Use "Gregory Kestin and his colleagues, at Harvard, found that a tutor could do better still when it was built on principles from research on teaching ...".
  - Line 120, "The difference between the two classroom results", now follows the Kosmyna preprint. Use "The difference between the chatbot and the tutors is the difference Bainbridge described."
  - Figure 14.6 is placed after line 120, three paragraphs after the sentence that cites it. Move the figure to follow line 114.
- **Line 90.** "the effect of an initial demanding task on a second one had an effect size of 0.04" repeats "effect". Use "an initial demanding task changed performance on a second one by an effect size of 0.04, essentially zero".
- **Lines 142 and 146.** The AI section now has two opening sentences, one for the figure and one for the principles. Merge them: "Figure 14.7 collects the chapter's verdicts on the evidence. Artificial intelligence has become, in a few years, one of the main tools with which people learn and work, and those verdicts suggest four principles for building it."
- **Line 24.** This is a one-sentence paragraph introducing Figure 14.2. Fold it into the end of line 22, after "and the next section begins with them."

Counts: Major 0, Minor 0, Copyedit 4.
