## Overall assessment

Both round-1 Majors and all nine Minors are in, and the chapter now works as the ending of the book.

- **The eight statements are now a summary a reader can recite.** The parallel openings (lines 32 to 46) read as a set: "the brain serves a body", "it is built from poor parts", "it predicts", "it learns locally and keeps globally", "it remembers on several clocks", "it turns value into action, and action into habit", "it is grown, not specified", "it is a compromise and an inheritance". Each now ends on one remembered image from an earlier chapter. Keller's mouse, the sutured-eye kitten, and the inverted retina sit naturally beside the thirst neurons, the dopamine window, the sea slug, and the rat at its lever.
- **The bookend is explicit.** The epigraph now matches the frontispiece, and the Central Lesson says so: "the one with which this book began" (line 146). The book opens and closes on the same sentence, and the last line ("to read that record by the principles that wrote it") lands with the whole book behind it.
- **The two added stories are the right ones.**
  - Sacks's L-dopa patients (line 88) bring the book's literary tradition into its last chapter. "had failed, been restored, and then overshot" is the dopamine part of the model told in people.
  - Freund and Kempermann's mice (line 110) make "must end up unique" something the reader has seen happen: "Same genes, same world, and still 40 different animals."
- **"One Afternoon, Every Level" now claims only what it shows** ("Nearly every statement", line 70), and remains the chapter's best demonstration of the model at work.
- **The callbacks are compressed.** Credit assignment and Gidon are brief recollections, and the H01 counts now match Figure 15.4. Figure 15.1 has left green behind.
- **The house style holds,** with no em dashes and no British spellings.

Prose quality: 5/5. Reader interest: 5/5.

### Copyedits (optional; do not affect the score)

- **Line 88 (recommended).** "the part of the model in which value becomes action" now appears twice in the same paragraph, in its first sentence and after Sacks. Change the second to "Value had stopped becoming action, been made to again, and then overshot", or to "The same part of the model had failed, been restored, and then overshot."
- **Lines 52 and 144.** "not separate facts but parts of one design" still appears in both places. Keep it in the Central Lesson, where it lands, and vary line 52: "What the model adds is the claim that they belong to one design."
- **Line 50.** The social-learning thread is a one-sentence paragraph between Figure 15.2 and the paragraph that explains how the statements fit together. Fold it into the start of line 52: "One thread runs through several of them: ... as Kuhl's infants showed in Chapter 14. None of the eight statements is new to this chapter; ..."
- **Line 96.** "Within five decades" should be "Within half a century", since FlyWire came 45 years after Crick's essay.
- **Line 60.** "The limited budget of attention described in Chapter 14 is spent within the hour" can read as a measurement. "is spent quickly" is safer.

Counts: Major 0, Minor 0, Copyedit 5.
