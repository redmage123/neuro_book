### Copyedits (sentence-level; fix in the copyedit pass)

- **Line 124.** "The settings of this system are shaped by experience, starting early. ... The stress system's settings, like perception's priors, are written by experience." The two sentences, two lines apart, say the same thing. Open the paragraph with "The settings of this system are learned, starting early." and keep the priors sentence as the payoff.
- **Line 26.** "flew into fits of rage at the slightest touch, hissing, with racing hearts, and named the display sham rage" Four commas stack up before the main verb returns. Rewrite: "found that cats whose cerebral cortex had been removed flew into fits of rage at the slightest touch, hissing, their hearts racing. They named the display sham rage, because it looked like anger without any sign of an object."
- **Line 158.** `"the animal passions of a strong man",` and `"no longer Gage".` American punctuation puts the comma and period inside the closing quotation mark: `"the animal passions of a strong man,"` and `"no longer Gage."`
- **Line 140.** "can be read as ways of correcting it." The antecedent is "errors of estimation", which is plural. Use "ways of correcting them".
