### Copyedits (optional; do not affect the score)

- **Line 144 (recommended).** "AlphaGo at move 78 had no such trigger and noticed its mistake only eight moves later, and many of its successors still lack one." There are three problems:
  - The count now reads from move 78, giving 86, while line 138 gives the mistake at 79 and the realization around 87.
  - "had no such trigger and noticed" half contradicts itself.
  - "one" has drifted away from "trigger".

  Line 138 already reports the delay, so the sentence need not repeat it. Use: "After move 78, AlphaGo's alarm came eight moves late; many of its successors still have none that comes in time." Or, shorter: "AlphaGo, answering move 78, had no such trigger, and many of its successors still lack one."
- **Line 92.** "lose the ability to learn at all ... no better than a network with no hidden layers at all" uses "at all" twice in one sentence. Drop the second.
- **Line 102.** "networks that learned this way learned faster" repeats the verb. Use "networks trained this way learned faster than with backpropagation and with less interference between tasks."
- **Line 142.** "One reason the brain may care about being right is that being wrong has cost it something" restates the sentence before it. Cut it (saves 18 words). The paragraph then runs straight from the body's pull toward accuracy to the model's contrasting reward.
- **Line 118.** The round-2 robot-hand compression is still available if words are needed: "Chapter 9's robot hand learned to turn a cube only after training in wildly randomized simulations" (saves about 10 words).

Counts: Major 0, Minor 0, Copyedit 5.
